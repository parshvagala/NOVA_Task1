What DJS NOVA is

A festival built for the genuinely curious

It started as a rooftop stargazing night for a handful of students with one borrowed telescope. It's grown into a two-day gathering for anyone who wants to understand the universe a little better — through talks, through building things, and through actually looking at it.

Keynote talks

Researchers, engineers and science communicators unpack the questions they spend their careers chasing.

Build & launch

Hands-on workshops on rocketry, optics and mission design. Bring a laptop, leave with a working prototype.

Night sky watch

Telescopes, red flashlights, and a clear-sky forecast we're cautiously optimistic about.
